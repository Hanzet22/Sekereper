Privacy Notice

HANZET-SCRAPER · Version 1 · Last updated: 28 October 2026
In short: use only public content you are authorized to access. Scraping sends the URL and request to the configured HANZET-SCRAPER API and the target website. Do not submit secrets or personal data you do not have permission to process.

Information processed

  • Target URLs and settings: URLs, rendering mode, crawl limits and job actions you submit are sent to the configured API.
  • Scraped results: responses may include text, metadata and other publicly served content from the target. Results are shown in your browser and can be exported by you.
  • Operational data: the server, hosting platform, network providers or security integrations may process request metadata such as IP address, timestamps, user agent, error details and usage needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Their own retention policies may apply.
  • Browser storage: this interface may store a versioned acknowledgement of this notice in local storage so it does not appear on every visit. It is not an account or authentication system.

How information is used

Information is processed to perform scraping/crawling you request, return results, maintain service reliability, enforce safety limits and investigate abuse. This project does not promise that hosting providers or target websites retain no request data.

Your responsibilities and choices

Do not submit credentials, session cookies, private URLs, confidential material or personal data unless you have a lawful basis and authorization. You can clear the site’s local storage to reset the acknowledgement. To avoid sending a target to the service, do not run a scrape or crawl.

Third parties

The deployed service may rely on Vercel or other configured infrastructure and, when enabled, external security providers. Their processing is governed by their respective notices. Proxy/VPN challenge functionality may be unavailable until configured.

Children, changes and contact

This is a technical utility, not a guarantee of legal compliance. The notice may change as the project changes. For questions or privacy requests, use the project’s GitHub issue tracker; do not post secrets or sensitive personal information publicly.
