Cookie Policy

HANZET-SCRAPER · Version 1 · Last updated: 29 October 2026
Short version: all categories are selectable. Choices start off until the visitor decides; “Reject all” is available alongside “Accept all”.

What this interface currently uses

The consent choice is stored in this browser's local storage, not in a cookie. The interface does not currently load analytics or advertising trackers. If the optional VPN/proxy challenge feature is configured and you complete the challenge, the server may issue a short-lived, signed security cookie to verify that challenge.

Categories

  • Essential / security: cookies or storage used for core functions and security. Turning this category off may make features that rely on a security cookie unavailable.
  • Functional: optional settings and convenience features.
  • Analytics: optional measurement of product usage; no analytics provider is currently enabled by this interface.
  • Marketing: optional advertising or cross-site marketing; no advertising tracker is currently enabled by this interface.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use “Cookie Preferences” in the footer to reopen the panel and save a new choice. You can also clear this site's local storage. Your preference applies to this browser only.

Important limitation

This manager records the user's choices. It does not automatically disable third-party cookies added outside this application's control. If analytics, advertising, or other providers are added later, their scripts and storage must be integrated so they are not activated before relevant consent is granted.
