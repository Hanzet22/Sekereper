Acceptable Use & Safety Notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026
Use responsibly: only scrape public resources you are authorized to access, and keep requests within the target website’s rules and reasonable limits.

Do not use this service to

  • Access private networks, internal services, localhost targets, or systems you do not have permission to test.
  • Bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, robots policies, or other access controls.
  • Collect or expose personal, confidential, sensitive, or credential data without proper authorization and a lawful basis.
  • Overload services, evade bans, harass people, distribute malware, or support unlawful activity.
  • Republish copyrighted material or violate contractual terms or intellectual-property rights.

Technical safeguards and limitations

The application reports that it restricts targets to public HTTP(S) destinations and applies configured limits. Safeguards can have limitations and are not a guarantee that every request is safe or lawful. VPN/proxy detection and challenge features may not be active in every deployment. Do not treat a challenge as permission to access a target.

Results and third-party sites

Scraped content comes from third-party sources and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or subject to separate restrictions. Verify important information at its source. Target site terms and applicable law still apply.

Availability and updates

The service is provided as a research utility and may change, fail, or be unavailable. Do not rely on it for critical tasks. These notices are informational and are not legal advice.
